# ☕ Coffeehouse

> A Telegram-native digital services platform designed for scalability, automation, community, and AI.

---

## Vision

Coffeehouse is not just a VPN store.

It is a modular platform for delivering digital services inside Telegram Mini Apps. The initial MVP focuses on VPN subscriptions, but the architecture is designed to support future products such as:

- AI Subscriptions
- Streaming Services
- Software Licenses
- Gaming Services
- Cloud Services
- Digital Accounts
- Gift Cards
- Third-party Marketplace Products
